---
name: What To answer means on the Email page
description: Ollie's definition of the To answer tab and why emails only become tasks when he acts
---
To answer = emails in Ollie's Gmail inbox, read or unread, that he has not replied to (last message not his). Not his own sent mail (that is Waiting for a reply), not archived mail. Newsletters, automatic emails and calendar invites are left out (his choice, 10th November 2026).

**Why:** Ollie said "To answer should only be emails in my inbox I have read or unread and not responded" after his own sent emails showed there as tasks.

**How to apply:** do not go back to listing email tasks on To answer. Reading an inbox email must not add to Tasks; a task is made only when he briefs, replies or saves an attachment. OllieOS cannot archive in Gmail, so Done is an OllieOS-side hide until a new message arrives.
